Cell respiration, week 4 notes
Glycolysis and the Krebs cycle
Glycolysis splits one glucose into two pyruvate in the cytoplasm and nets 2 ATP.
Pyruvate enters the mitochondria, where the Krebs cycle releases carbon dioxide and loads electron carriers.
The electron transport chain uses those carriers and oxygen to make most of the ATP.
Without oxygen, cells fall back on fermentation. It makes far less ATP per glucose.
Each stage hands energy to the next, so a gap in one shows up in all that follow it.
